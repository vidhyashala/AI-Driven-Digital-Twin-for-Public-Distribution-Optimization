# AstraKshetra Nexus Research Brief

AstraKshetra Nexus is a patent-oriented PDS-DigitalTwin framework combining executable public-distribution twins, entitlement-preserving optimization, federated rural intelligence, blockchain verification, and explainable autonomous governance.

## Research hypothesis

A real-time cyber-physical twin with AI-assisted synchronization and deprivation-weighted optimization can reduce stock-outs, detect leakage earlier, improve disaster resilience, and preserve auditability at national scale.

## Minimum viable research demonstrator

1. District graph with shops, warehouses, trucks, and route risk.
2. Event-sourced twin assimilation from IoT/ePOS/GPS streams.
3. PAEF entitlement-failure risk tensor.
4. RL-ready allocation environment.
5. Explainable allocation recommendations.
